Introduction:  Noise  is  a  reality  in  most  industrial  activities,  being  able  to  cause  effects beyond  hearing  loss,  as  headache,  sleep  disturbance,  changes  in  body  balance,  blood pressure  disorders,  stomach  disturbs,  stress  and other  emotional  problems  such  as  low tolerance,  anxiety,  isolation,  isolation  in  family  communications,  low  self-esteem  and depression.  Objective:  To  investigate  the  non-auditory  effects  of  exposed  workers  to noise in a footwear production segment. Methods: A descriptive, observational and cross-sectional study was conducted with a quantitative approach. It was analyzed data of 392 workers, of both sexes, of a footwear company located in the Metropolitan Region of João Pessoa/PB. The data were obtained through the Noise Protocol in Occupational Health by  Santos,  Andrade  and  Soares  (2021).  Results:  The  most  cited  extra-auditory  effects were  anxiety  (77.80%),  physical  fatigue  (75.51%),  mental  fatigue  (67.34%),  headache (63.38%),  irritation  (63.01%)  and  stress (61.73%).  Conclusion:  It  was  noticed  a  great prevalence  of  non-auditory  effects  in  workers  exposed  to  noise.  It  shows  the  need  for greater occupational health and safety, such as the application of control standards, such as  greater  worker  awareness,  has  not  been  related  to  the  use  of  personal  protective equipment for occupational health optimization.

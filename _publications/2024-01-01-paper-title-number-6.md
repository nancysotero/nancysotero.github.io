Book chapter, in Portuguese (Interface entre Música e Audiologia, em intervenção fonoaudiológica em audição e linguagem).

The relationship between humanity and music has been analyzed and explained from various perspectives throughout the millennia. Whether from an aesthetic or scientific point of view, efforts have been made to understand why music, defined as the simultaneous or successive organization of a sequence of sounds over time, with order and balance, has such an impact on human beings. In this chapter, we will address the interface between music and hearing, emphasizing the response of the auditory cortex, the behavioral benefits of hearing, and the principles of auditory rehabilitation using music.

Book chapter, in Portuguese (Zumbido: uma introdução geral, em Multidisciplinaridade a favor do Zumbido).

This work was developed based on the First Multidisciplinary Meeting on Tinnitus held at the Federal University of Paraíba (UFPB), organized by the Study and Research Group on Hearing, Balance, and Tinnitus (GEPAEZ) and collaborators. Thus, through the compilation of chapters on the role of various health and related fields in addressing the symptom of tinnitus, the aim is to contribute to fostering studies and research on the topic, since tinnitus is a symptom that affects a large portion of the population and significantly impairs the quality of life of those affected.
